Research

PhD & academic work on dependable PLC software and formal approaches.

Focus

PhD & academic

My doctoral work targets dependable PLC software: specifying expected behaviour, monitoring at runtime, and connecting to industrial toolchains— including transpilation from Structured Text (ST) to C where it improves integration, portability, or analysis.

Runtime verification for PLCs

Framework design and evaluation for programmable logic controllers, with emphasis on safe program organization units and experiments that say something meaningful about real-world control logic.

Specifications that fit practice

Using C as a specification language where it aligns with workflows engineers already reason about; tooling that bridges academic models and shop-floor constraints.

Formal structure for learning

RMLGym connects reward machines and reinforcement learning—bringing formal, property-based structure into RL settings (see publications).

Evidence & rigour

Literature foundations in formal verification and RV; experimental methodology that backs claims with reproducible artefacts where possible.
