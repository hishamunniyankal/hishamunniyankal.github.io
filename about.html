Biography

I am a doctoral researcher in computer science with an MSc specialization in Data Science and AI. My academic AI work includes reinforcement learning, computer vision, explainable classification, speech processing, and natural language processing. My current doctoral research in runtime verification has strengthened my experience in formal reasoning, research-software development, experimentation, and system evaluation.

Before beginning the PhD, I worked on production software and large-scale security-data systems. This engineering background supports my ability to turn research ideas into implementable, testable and maintainable systems.

I am interested in academic and industrial AI research, AI research engineering, and applied machine-learning opportunities. Detailed project descriptions are on the Research page; roles and employment history are on Experience.
