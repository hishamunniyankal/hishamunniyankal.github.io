Biography

I am a doctoral researcher in Computer Science at the University of Genoa (DIBRIS). My current research focuses on runtime verification and runtime monitoring for dependable industrial control software, particularly programmable logic controllers (PLCs). I design and implement research software for monitor generation and evaluation, and study monitor behaviour under practical controller constraints.

My academic background also includes applied machine learning, including reinforcement learning and experimental computer-vision projects. That work sits alongside the doctoral research rather than replacing it: it shows how I approach learning-based experiments, evaluation and research software in academic settings.

Before the PhD, I worked on production software and large-scale security-data systems. That engineering experience continues to shape a research engineering perspective—turning research ideas into implementable, testable and maintainable systems. Detailed projects are on the Research page; roles and dates are on Experience.
