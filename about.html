Biography

I am a doctoral researcher in Computer Science at the University of Genoa. My current research focuses on runtime verification and research software for dependable industrial control systems, particularly programmable logic controllers (PLCs).

My background also includes academic applied machine learning, including reinforcement learning and related experimental projects. Before the PhD, I worked on production software and large-scale security-data systems, which gives me a useful research engineering perspective when turning research ideas into implementable systems.
