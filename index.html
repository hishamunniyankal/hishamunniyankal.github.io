Research

My work explores how machines can acquire, represent, and apply knowledge in a manner that is interpretable and trustworthy. I am particularly interested in the following directions:

Knowledge-Augmented LLMs

Designing retrieval-augmented generation pipelines that ground large language model outputs in verified knowledge sources, reducing hallucinations.

Graph Reasoning

Developing neural-symbolic methods for multi-hop reasoning over heterogeneous knowledge graphs, combining embedding-based and logic-based approaches.

Explainability & Trust

Creating post-hoc and intrinsic explanation methods for NLP models, with applications in high-stakes domains such as healthcare and legal text analysis.

Low-Resource Learning

Investigating few-shot and zero-shot transfer techniques for under-resourced languages, enabling equitable access to language technology.
