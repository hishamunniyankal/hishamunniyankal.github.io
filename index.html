Profile

I am a doctoral researcher at the University of Genoa (DIBRIS). My research focuses on runtime verification and formal methods for programmable logic controller (PLC) systems, with an emphasis on making verification usable in realistic software and controller workflows.

Before and alongside research, I built software across security analytics, data systems, and full-stack web products. That experience continues to shape how I design and evaluate research methods and tools.
